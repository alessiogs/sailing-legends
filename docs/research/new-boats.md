# New boats research: ILCA 4/6/7, 29er, 49er (49erFX)

Tags: [P] primary (class association, World Sailing, federation), [S] secondary (Wikipedia, builders, forums, theses, press), [G] guess or derived by me (flagged). Researched 2026-11-03. No repo was modified.

Summary of the data situation: hull/rig/weight facts are well sourced. Measured boat speeds, TWA and VMG per wind speed are NOT published in any accessible source. Only the 49er class publishes race-committee pace tables (VMG per wind band). Everything else in section 4 is derived or a guess and flagged.

### Who sails which rig, ages

- UK (class youth rep): boys aiming at international level sail ILCA 4 up to U16, move to ILCA 6 at 15-16, and most move to ILCA 7 at 17-18. Girls often move to ILCA 6 a year later than boys, and ILCA 6 is the biggest rig girls use. [S] https://ilca.uk/choosing-your-rig
- ILCA 4 suits young sailors coming from Optimist or RS Tera who are too light for a standard ILCA. [S] Wikipedia.
- Italy, FIV 2019 youth regulation (old, it still says "Laser 4.7"): Optimist and Laser 4.7 are single-handed classes for the "Cadetti-Juniores" 11-15 band (born 2004-2008 at the time), with Laser 4.7 mandatory for some birth years (2007-2008 "must sail singles"). U19 reference classes (born 2001 and later at the time): 420 M/F, Laser Radial M/F, RS:X Youth, 29er M/F, Nacra 15, Kite. The 29er and 420 are the youth double-handers. The 49er is not mentioned. [P] https://www.federvela.it/images/fiv/normativa_giovanile_2019_20_dic_18.pdf
- Italy 2026 (zone XV training call): ILCA 4 has an "U16" category (M/F); ILCA 6 has "Under 19" crews, mixed and female. Birth-year cutoffs are not stated in the call. [P] https://zona15.federvela.it/wp-content/uploads/sites/17/2026/05/RTZ-ILCA-4-e-6-Dervio-22-24-Giu-2026.pdf
- Not found: an explicit FIV "U17" rule for ILCA, and the 2026 FIV birth-year tables (the Italian federation "Schede classe" 2026 at federvela.it exist but only 420/Feva/etc. surfaced). [G] Typical Italian path: Optimist to age 15, then ILCA 4 (U16) at 13-15, ILCA 6 at 15-19 (U19), ILCA 7 from 17-19+. This matches the UK pattern, but I did not find the Italian source stating it.

## 4. Speeds

### 4.1 49er: the only class-published performance data

49er class "Target Time" tables (race-committee pace, minutes per nautical mile of leg length, so this is VMG, not boat speed). They are targets for race committees, not measured polars, but they come from the class. DCJ version 10, March 2019, read from https://old.49er.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/04/49er-speed-chart-1.pdf [P]; the table rows imply (1 nm leg, Up/Down minutes): 5-8 kn 15.0/10.0; 8-12 kn 12.0/7.0; 12-15 kn 9.5/5.5; 15+ kn 10.0/5.5.

Version 9 (July 2016) https://49er.org/wp-content/uploads/2018/11/Target-Time-for-49er.pdf [P]: up 15/11/9/9; run 11/7/6/5.5 min/nm (the file header and its table disagree slightly on the 15+ run).

49erFX (https://old.49er.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/04/49erFX-Speed-Chart.pdf [P], garbled column headings, so [G] on the exact mapping): up 16/13/11/10; run 12/8/7/6 min/nm.

Converted to VMG (kn = 60 / (min/nm)), 49er v10:

| True wind band    | VMG up (kn) | VMG down (kn) |
| ----------------- | ----------- | ------------- |
| 5-8 (use for 7)   | 4.0         | 6.0           |
| 8-12 (use for 11) | 5.0         | 8.6           |
| 12-15             | 6.3         | 10.9          |
| 15+ (use for 16)  | 6.0         | 10.9          |

Boat speed needs an angle. No source gives 49er TWA. [G] Assuming upwind TWA 42-45 deg (about 84-90 deg tack) and downwind VMG TWA 150 deg at 7 kn, 142 deg at 11 kn, 140 deg at 16 kn, the derived boat speeds are:

| Wind  | Upwind            | Reaching (TWA ~90-100) | Downwind best VMG   |
| ----- | ----------------- | ---------------------- | ------------------- |
| 7 kn  | 5.4 kn at TWA ~43 | [G] ~8                 | 6.9 kn at TWA ~150  |
| 11 kn | 6.7 kn at TWA ~42 | [G] ~13-14             | 10.9 kn at TWA ~142 |
| 16 kn | 8.1 kn at TWA ~42 | [G] ~17-19             | 14.2 kn at TWA ~140 |

All of this table is [G] derived. "Over 30 kn" top speed [S] heppell.net. Mean velocity and VMG both rise with wind speed in 49er/49erFX GPS data (Hyeres 2018 World Cup, 5.5-11.6 kn wind for 49er, 5 Hz TracTrac), and downwind values exceed upwind ones, but the paper text has no absolute speeds or TWA. [S] https://www.cambridge.org/core/product/4336D8F027A475ED8F02CA438DC2A54B/core-reader

| ----- | ---------------- | --------- | ---------------------------------- |

Only qualitative anchors: "will often exceed the wind speed when planing" [S] Wikipedia. Test sailing on Attersee (polar windows 6-12 kn) exists but the speed values are in figures only [S] univie thesis.

### 4.3 ILCA (no published polars found)

The only quantitative item: a Chalmers thesis on the Laser upwind at TWA 45 deg, apparent wind angle 27-30 deg, wind up to about 11 kn, top speed 5 to 5.4 kn. [S] https://core.ac.uk/download/80717962.pdf . Boat speed vs TWS was not tabulated in the readable part.

| ----- | ---------------------- | ----------------- | ------------------- |
| 7 kn  | ~3.6 kn                | ~5 kn             | ~4.5 kn at TWA ~150 |
| 11 kn | ~4.4 kn                | ~6.5 kn (surfing) | ~5.5 kn at TWA ~150 |
| 16 kn | ~4.6 kn (hiking limit) | ~8 kn (planing)   | ~6.5 kn at TWA ~145 |

### 4.4 Reference classes (repo targets, not researched)

The project already has: Optimist 11 kn about 2.3 kn upwind, 3.9 reaching; 420 about 4 kn upwind, 6.5 reaching, 8-9 at 16 kn. Not re-sourced. Do not mix: the ILCA guess above (4.4 kn upwind at 11 kn) is a little above the 420 target (4 kn), consistent with a single-hander of similar hull speed. Check that the 420 target is not exceeded by the ILCA 4 or 6.

## 6. Uncertainties and conflicts

1. Speed data: no polar, GPS table or VPP found for ILCA, 29er or 49er. Apart from the 49er VMG pace tables (class-issued, race-committee targets), every speed and TWA above is my estimate. Best next step: a GPS/TracTrac export (49er/29er event logs), or the Chalmers/ILCA performance literature, or the polars in a tool such as SailGP/Sailing Analytics.
2. 49er tables are VMG, race-committee paces; the extracted PDF columns were garbled, so mappings (especially FX) are partly inferred. The 15+ kn band is slower upwind than 12-15 kn in the v10 table (probably waves/depowering).
3. 29er: length 4.40 vs 4.45 m; upwind area 12.4-12.5 vs 13.19 m2; gennaker 15.0 vs 16.83 m2 (the class page's own 181.2 sq ft equals 16.83 m2). Spinnaker area conflicts need the class rules measurement diagram (the rules PDF doesn't contain areas; the sail specs sit in the builder manual).
4. 49er: length 4.876 / 4.9 / 4.99 m; upwind area 19.97 vs 21.2 m2; gennaker 37.16 vs 38 m2.
5. ILCA: weight ranges differ by source (e.g. ILCA 4: 40-60, 45-60, 50-65 kg). Hull weight 57 (class min, from memory) vs 59 kg (Wikipedia). The 2026 class rules PDF could not be opened, so sail areas and mast sections are [S].
6. Ages: UK pathway is documented [S]. The Italian FIV pathway is only visible in the 2019 youth regulation (U19 classes: 420, Laser Radial, 29er) and a 2026 zone call (ILCA 4 = U16, ILCA 6 = U19). The 2026 FIV birth-year tables and any "U17" detail were not found. No source for 49er entry age.
7. The 29er gybe-angle and planing-onset questions have no source at all. The RRS 42 29er guide at https://pya.org.pl/files/dzial-sportu/sedziowie/dok/RRS-42-Guide-29er.pdf was surfaced by search but not read, and might show gybing technique, not angles.
