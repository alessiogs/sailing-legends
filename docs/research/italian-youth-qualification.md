# Italian youth dinghy qualification (Optimist and 420)

Purpose: ground the career-mode season and qualification design in how Italian youth sailors really progress.
Researched 2026-10-29. Terms follow the Italian sources; English glosses in brackets.

**Confidence key:** [P] primary source read directly (FIV document, class conditions, a notice of race). [S] secondary
(club news, press). [H] historic (2017 or 2018 documents, so the numbers have likely moved).

**Gap that matters:** the current FIV *Schede di classe* for Optimist and 420 (the documents that give the exact
selection mechanics) could not be fetched; see "Not confirmed" at the end. Section 3 and 4 therefore mix the 2026
general rules [P] with 2017/2018 class rules [H] and 2026 event notices [P].

## 1. Age limits and categories

**Optimist (international rule) [P].** The IODA Optimist World and Continental Championship conditions say: "Any boy or
girl may enter until 31st December of the year in which he or she attains the age of 15 years" (World Championship
conditions 9.2; Continental conditions 10.2), and a sailor old enough to start may finish.
Source: https://www.ct-sailing.org.tw/media/k2/attachments/2024_Optimist_handbook_Document_2990_20240201194100_en.pdf
(copy of the 2024 IODA/World Sailing Optimist handbook). The class rules themselves only require one person aboard and
membership of a national Optimist association: https://www.ussailing.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/08/IODA-Class-Rules-2020.pdf

**FIV general rules 2026 [P].** Minimum age is "the year of the eleventh birthday"; category change is tied to "the
year of reference for each category" (so it is by birth year, not birthday). Class categories follow international
determinations. Source: https://www.federvela.it/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/Normative-per-lattivita-sportiva-nazionale-organizzata-in-Italia_2026.pdf
(Premessa, p. 5-6, approved 18/12/2025).

**FIV Campionato Italiano Giovanile categories 2026 [P]:** single-handed Under 13, 15, 16, 17, 18, 19 (Optimist,
Techno 293, ILCA 4, ILCA 6, O'Pen Skiff, iQFoil); double-handed (420) Under 19, 17, 15 and Assoluto Mixed (FIV general
rules p. 39-40). The Optimist Cadetti/Juniores split is the class's own Italian structure.

**420 age limits [P].** International 420 Junior European Championship (2022 notice): every crew member must not have
reached their 19th birthday on or before 31 December of the year (born 2004 or later in 2022); U17 division: not 17 by
31 December (born 2006 or later); U15 trophy for crews born 2008 or later. Entry quotas per nation: 10 boats Junior, 5
U17, 15 total (20/10/30 for the host). Source: https://420sailing.org/uploaded_files/Document_1815_20220428100641_en.pdf
Italian Campionato Italiano 420 2026 is described in press as for athletes "between 14 and 19" [S]:
https://www.unionesarda.it/sport-sardegna/la-vela-giovanile-accendera-la-gallura-a-cannigione-il-campionato-italiano-classe-420-nxckfwn5
2018 FIV note [H]: 420 sailors born 2006 were admitted from the 12th year; medical certificate type B (agonistica)
required for nationals and championships, type A for zonal-only activity.

## 2. Season calendar structure

### Optimist (two parallel tracks, Juniores and Cadetti)

Pipeline for Juniores, in time order (2017/2018 class documents [H]; 2026 notices confirm the interzonal and
championship stages exist [P]):

1. **Selezioni Zonali (SZ)**, inside each FIV Zona, spring-summer. 2017: first mandatory on 14 May, second by 22-23
   July; open entry; max 9 races, 3 per day; valid with 2 races (2017) or 4 (a 2023 Zona XIV notice). They feed the
   Campionato Italiano and the Coppe (Div. A to Campionato Italiano and Coppa del Presidente; Div. B to Coppa Primavela
   and Coppa Cadetti, per the 2023 Zona XIV notice). A Zona may also run a longer zonal championship of several
   weekends (2017 Marche: 7 race days, valid with 9 races, discards of the 4th, 9th, 14th, 19th race, only two
   weekends counted for the SZ): https://leganavaleancona.altervista.org/wp-content/uploads/2017/02/Bando-Campionato-Zonale-OPTIMIST-2017.pdf
2. **Selezioni Interzonali (SI)**, March. Several grouped Zone sets. 2026: Genoa, 19-22 March, Zones I, XIV, XV, Div. A
   born 2011-2015, max 9 races, max 3 a day, valid with 3 races, one discard from race 4, low-point scoring (RRS A4),
   prizes top 5 overall and top 5 girls (notice above). YCI reports 42 places from this event for the National
   Selection: https://www.yachtclubitaliano.it/en/news/Selezione%20Interzonale%20Optimist [S]. 2017 variant: a further
   "Regata di Selezione" (max 6 races) open only to SI entrants [H].
3. **Selezioni Nazionali (SN)**, April-June, usually two events. 2017: 1st SN 160 helms from the SI (9 races), 2nd SN
   top 120 of the 1st (up to 12 races, max 21 in total across the two, final ranking over both). 2018: 140 admitted to the
   1st SN, 2nd SN with a Gold fleet of 70 boats [H]. The press described a 2022 SN as the "first national selection for
   the European and World championships", open to sailors aged 11-14 who qualified at four interzonals the previous
   weekend: https://www.quotidianodiragusa.it/2022/04/02/appuntamenti/marina-di-ragusa-selezione-nazionale-per-campionato-europeo-e-mondiale-della-classe-optimist/ [S]
4. **Campionato Italiano Giovanile (single-handed)**, late August-early September. 2026: Marina di Ravenna, 29 Aug-1
   Sept, about 150 Optimist helms: https://www.ilrestodelcarlino.it/ravenna/sport/campionati-italiani-di-vela-la-fc237692 [S];
   FIV rules say Optimist has 150 reserved places "selected by class rules" (p. 39).
5. **Coppe for the younger birth years**: Coppa Primavela, Coppa Cadetti, Coppa del Presidente, 80 places each per
   birth year (FIV 2026 p. 38 [P]; 2017/2018 identical [H]).
6. **Club-team championship** (Campionato Italiano a Squadre di Club): max 30 teams in 2017 (2 per Zona), team score is
   the sum of the club's top 3 helms in the two SN [H]. YCI notes a 2026 team qualification:
   https://www.yachtclubitaliano.it/en/news/our-optimist-team-qualifies-for-the-italian-team-championship [S, not read in detail].
7. **International**: European and World Championships plus team events, filled by the Italian national team picked from
   the SN results. Quote: 12 athletes selected for Europeans/Worlds (YCI interzonal article above; wording ambiguous on
   whether this is from the SI or SN). In 2017 the Campionato Giovanile added 12 helms "selected for Worlds/Europeans"
   on top of 132 qualified Italians [H].

Open non-selection regattas also exist and are what a family fills the calendar with: Meeting del Garda (Riva, early
April, Juniores 10 races over 3 qualifying days plus a finals day, Cadetti 8, one discard from 4 completed races; the
notice states no selection value) (link in table above), Trofeo Arilica (3 races, no discard), Meringa Cup (max 6
races, discard from 4):
https://www.campioneunivela.it/site/assets/files/34642/bando_di_regata_meringa_cup_2026_italiano.pdf ;
https://docs.google.com/document/d/1PIhLjPYvO5ZVfu_R22PFXqikxE-Wl7P/export?format=pdf (Trofeo Arilica 2025 notice).

### 420 (national ranking circuit, then championships)

- **Zonal regattas first [H].** 2018: to enter the Campionato Italiano Giovanile (U19-U17 and Assoluto) a crew needed at
  least 2 zonal regattas (3 races each) before 1 August; direct access for the reigning champion and the podiums of the
  last junior Europeans/Worlds.
- **National ranking events** (Coppa Italia 420 stages, Regate Nazionali 420, Coppa UNIQUA). Examples:
  - 2016: Regata Nazionale 3 in Sanremo (3 races) and Ranking Event 4 in Ravenna, points counted toward World and Junior
    European selection: https://420sailing.org/default/news/new/text/italian-ranking-event-puts-teams-closer-to-420-world-and-junior-european-championship-selection [S]
  - A later season: three national regattas (Anzio, Cagliari, Trieste), 22 races in total, whose final ranking chose the
    World (4 YCI crews in the team story) and Junior European crews:
    https://www.yachtclubitaliano.it/en/news/national-selections-for-the-upcoming-420-world-and-european-championships-conclude-yci-scores-a-winning-hand [S]
  - A Coppa Italia season: stage III Livorno 10 races, 102 crews, the series totalling 32 races, stage III secured 3
    World spots and 1 European spot, the rest decided at the Coppa Uniqua in Cesenatico in early June:
    https://www.yachtclubitaliano.it/en/news/III%20Tappa%20Coppa%20Italia%20420%20%E2%80%93%20Livorno [S]
- **Campionato Italiano**: 2026 had two 420 championships: the Campionato Italiano classe 420 in Cannigione (Sardinia),
  mid-March, over 100 crews (unionesarda link above [S]); and the FIV Campionato Italiano Giovanile in Doppio
  (Manfredonia, 5-10 September in the year YCI reported): https://www.yachtclubitaliano.it/en/news/Campionati%20Italiani%20Giovanili [S].
  FIV 2026 lists the doubles championship categories U19, U17, U15, Assoluto Mixed (p. 40 [P]).
- **International**: 420 Worlds and Junior European Championship, normally held July (examples above), chosen from the
  national ranking.

## 3. Qualification: what qualifies for what

**General FIV rule 2026 [P].** "Places at the class International Championships are assigned on the basis of the
National Ranking List" (this applies to Optimist and 420; up to two places per category are reserved for FIV-called-up
crews only in the Olympic-pathway youth classes, which Optimist and 420 are not). Only races held in Italy count
(FIV p. 31, 35-36). Class associations must publish their ranking parameters by **31 December of the previous year** (p.
31-32), so the ranking-year rules are fixed before the season starts, and the points all come from the same season.

**Ranking points formula [P].** Ranking Points = E (N - P + 1) / N, where N is the number of competitors, P the
position and E a quality factor set by the class; E is 40 (the maximum) for FIV Youth Championships; if an event with
E >= 20 fails to complete three races the points halve; fewer than two races means E = 0. Youth categories are computed
"by highlighting", meaning athletes outside the category are removed from the order first (p. 31-34). The unified system
is optional for youth classes.

**Optimist [H].** Same-season chain: Zonal selection (open) -> Interzonal (quota by Zone) -> Nazionale 1 -> Nazionale 2
(top 120 carry on) -> Campionato Giovanile. Each stage only admits from the stage before, within the same calendar
year, and Italy's Europeans/Worlds team is drawn from the national selections. There was no Optimist ranking list in
2018 [H]; check whether the 2026 class card has one.

**Quota per Zona [H].** Seats are shared in proportion to the number of registrants per Zona at a cut-off date:
- Campionato Giovanile: 120 x (Zona entries / total entries on 7 May) (2017); 140 places "calculated per Zona" with a
  20 May reference date (2018).
- Coppe: 80 places per birth year, same logic (rounding can give more than 80; FIV makes the final list).
- 1st Interzonal admission: 160 x (SI entries / total entries on 19 March), e.g. 505 entries: I-XIV-XV 40, II-III-IV 37,
  V-IX 43, X-XIII 40 (2017). 2018 used 140 and a note says the table's total (160) disagrees with the formula.
  Sources: both optimist-it.com PDFs (links in the table and the 420 section). 2026 FIV text has no Zona quotas, only a
  ferry-cost refund for Zones III, VII and Isole Minori.

**Trofeo CONI 2026 [P]** (regional teams, ages 11-14): the Optimist seat goes to the first athlete not already chosen in
the overall ranking of the AICO Optimist zonal selection races; Optimist born 2013-2015, RS Feva 2012-2015; 3 athletes
per region; best of 6 races, 3 a day, one discard from 4 races:
https://www.coni.it/images/Trofeo_CONI/2026/estivo/FIV_Regolamento_Tecnico_Trofeo_CONI_2026.pdf (this confirms that
the *zonal* results are the ranking of record).

**420 [H].** 2018: Worlds: top 6 male, top 6 female, top 3 U17 male, top 3 U17 female plus the Coppa UNIQUA winner,
excluding crews already selected; Junior Europeans: top 7 crews with at least 2 female and the top 3 U17; foreign crews
are excluded from the national rankings; selection also requires taking part in the national regattas and being on the
ranking list; a split crew keeps 50% of ranking points each. Source: https://www.optimist-it.com/admin/_static/files/testi/152214085582800/152214096442730.pdf
(FIV 2018 norms part two, with unresolved editorial notes). Same season: the Worlds/Europeans of July are chosen from
that spring's events (Coppa Italia stages up to the Uniqua in early June).

## 4. Regattas per season, races per regatta, scoring and discards

**Scoring [P].** Low-point (Minimum Points, RRS Appendix A) for all classes (FIV 2026 p. 22). Max 3 races a day unless
a class card allows 4; one 4th race can be added once per event if needed to validate it (p. 22-23). A title needs at
least 3 valid races and, for fleet championships, at least 15 boats per class and category (p. 22).

**Discards [P].** 1-3 races: none; 4 or more: one discard; the notice of race may differ (FIV 2026 p. 23). Historic
variants: a 2nd discard from the 9th race only if the class asks and FIV recommends not to for ranking events (2018 [H]);
the 2017 SN final ranking used none up to 5 races, one for 6-11, two for 12-21 [H].

**Regattas per season (inference, not stated by any one document).** An Optimist Junior following the full pipeline
sails roughly: 2 zonal selections, 1 interzonal, 2 national selections, the Campionato (and for some, a Worlds/Europeans
team event), i.e. 6-7 selection events, about 60-70 races, plus 3-6 open regattas in autumn and winter. A 420 crew
sails 2 zonal regattas, 3-4 national ranking stages, the championship, and, if selected, an international in July.

## 5. Design hints for the game

These are interpretations, not sourced facts.
- Model the season as stages with a quota that shrinks (Zona 100+ -> interzonal 160 -> SN 120 -> Campionato ~150).
- Use a birth-year window and a "last year" flag at the age-15 cutoff (31 Dec of the year of the 15th birthday).
- Scoring: low-point, one discard from the 4th race, max 3 races a day; valid event needs 3 races.
- Qualification is within the same season, with parameters fixed by 31 December of the previous year.
- 420 ranking = national ranking events within the season, picking fixed numbers per category (open, girls, U17).
